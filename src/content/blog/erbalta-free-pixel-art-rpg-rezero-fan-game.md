## What Is Erbalta? A Village That Relives the Same Dawn (◕‿◕)

Subaru and Emilia arrive in **Erbalta**, a quiet village where nobody grows old... and nobody ever leaves. The Sage spends his days watching the trees grow, but the trees never get any taller. The sign in the square says *"Do not leave the village."* And in the library, a black book with blank pages has one sentence written on the last one, in handwriting you know very well: **yours**.

Talk to the villagers, find out what the black book remembers, and cross the **Timeless Wood**, where something never closes its eyes.

*When Subaru dies, he goes back. The world forgets. He doesn't.*

## Return by Death as a Game Mechanic (ー_ー〃)

If you know Re:Zero, you know the hook: Subaru doesn't get extra lives, he gets **memories**. I wanted the game to work the same way.

When you die in Erbalta, you go back to your last point of return. The world resets: enemies, items, conversations. But some characters notice that something is off, and Subaru keeps what he learned. The final boss is built around this: the first time, you probably won't survive its Gaze. The second time, Subaru *remembers*, and the battle opens with a hint that wasn't there before.

Dying isn't a game over screen. It's how you learn.

## Turn-Based Battles: Weaknesses, Shields and Emilia (b^_^)b

The combat is a small turn-based system inspired by the "break" mechanics of modern JRPGs:

* Every enemy has a **shield** and hidden **weaknesses** (punch, kick, shadow, ice)
* Hit a weakness to crack the shield; break it and the enemy is **stunned** and takes double damage
* In the Timeless Wood, **Emilia joins the party** with her frost magic
* Together you unlock a combo attack, **Shattered Crystal**, and a final ultimate against the boss

## Made With Godot 4: A Learning Project (✧ω✧)

Erbalta started as a way to **learn Godot 4** one step at a time: scenes, signals, tweens, shaders, particles, save files, translations. Every feature was an excuse to understand a new piece of the engine.

A few things I'm quietly proud of:

* Shaders for fog, sun rays, cloud shadows, a rewind effect and a glitch that tears a window in half
* A save system, a "point of return" system and progress that survives a new game
* **224 automated tests** with gdUnit4, including the balance simulator and a check that every line of text has its English translation

The whole project is open source, with a development diary (in Italian) that explains every step, mistakes included:
**[github.com/biagio-scaglia/let-s-learn-godot-](https://github.com/biagio-scaglia/let-s-learn-godot-)**

## FAQ (・ω・)ノ

**Is Erbalta free?**
Yes. It's a free, non-commercial fan game: no price, no ads, no microtransactions.

**Is Erbalta an official Re:Zero game?**
No. It's an unofficial fan game. Subaru and Emilia belong to Tappei Nagatsuki and Kadokawa. Erbalta tells a new, original story with them.

**Do I need to know Re:Zero to play?**
Not at all. The story stands on its own. If you know the series, you'll catch the references, starting with "Emilia-tan".

**What platforms does it run on?**
Windows, as a free download on itch.io.

**Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?**
The game isn't digitally signed, so Windows shows that warning for any small indie executable. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**What engine is it made with?**
Godot 4.7, with GDScript. The source code is on GitHub.
