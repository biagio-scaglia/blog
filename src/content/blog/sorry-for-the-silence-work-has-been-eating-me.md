Hey, cyberspace. (ｍ＿＿ｍ)

I owe you an apology.

This blog went quiet for a stretch. No new diary entries, no framework announcements, no late-night rambling about handhelds or anime soundtracks. The RSS feed just… sat there. And if you checked in expecting a new log and found nothing, I’m genuinely sorry.

## The Short Version (￣ヘ￣)

**Work has been really, really stressful.**

Not the fun kind of stressful — not “deadline adrenaline and a clean merge.” The heavy kind. The kind where your calendar fills itself, Slack never sleeps, and every “quick sync” quietly steals another hour from the evening you planned to write here.

```
       [ Webmaster Status ]
       ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
       │ Blog drafting:        IDLE       │
       │ Day job CPU:          97%        │
       │ Sleep buffer:         CRITICAL   │
       │ Apology queue:        FLUSHING… │
       └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

I kept telling myself I’d write “this weekend.” Then the weekend became catch-up tickets. Then catch-up tickets became “I’ll just rest my eyes for ten minutes.” You know the loop. (ー_ー〃)

## Not Gone — Just Overloaded ( ﾟ，_ゝ｀)

I didn’t abandon this place. Biagio Cyberspace is still home. I still open the Astro project when I can. I still think about posts while waiting for builds at work. But when your social battery and your creative battery both hit zero at the same time, the diary becomes the first process you kill.

That doesn’t feel fair to the people who actually read these logs. So: **I’m sorry for the absence.** Thank you for sticking around the guestbook, the shoutbox, and the old posts while I was offline in spirit.

## What’s Next (◡‿◡✿)

I’m not promising a daily posting schedule — that would just become another stressor. I *am* promising that this corner of the web stays alive. More personal logs. More open-source notes. More retro handheld nonsense. One entry at a time, when the system has a spare cycle.

If you’re also buried under work right now: you’re not weak for needing silence. Reboots take time.

Until the next log — with a clearer head, hopefully.

— Biagio (ｍ＿＿ｍ)
